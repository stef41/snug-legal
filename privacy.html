Snug Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Snug is a cozy home for two people. We built it to be as private as possible: we do not run accounts or store readable copies of your data, we do not collect analytics, we do not show ads, and we do not track you.

What we do not collect

  • No email, phone number, or password.
  • No name, birthdate, or profile of any kind on our servers.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising IDs or analytics identifiers.
  • No behavioral or usage data.

What is stored, and where

Everything you do in Snug (your mood, your notes, your doodle, your bonsai, your pet, your photos, your countdowns) lives on your own device. It is written to your device's local storage and, for photos, its private database. We never see it.

How syncing works

To keep your device in sync with your partner's, Snug uses its dedicated encrypted relay hosted on Cloudflare Workers (the default), or any other ntfy-compatible server you choose in the More tab. Before anything leaves your device, it is encrypted with a key derived from your private couple code using AES-256-GCM. The relay only ever sees encrypted bytes; it cannot read your messages, and neither can we.

Your couple code is never sent to any server. It exists only on the two devices that know it.

Photos

Photos you choose to share are downscaled to about 640 pixels, compressed, and encrypted on your device before traveling through the relay so your partner can receive them even while you are offline. The relay only sees encrypted ciphertext and automatically drops older blobs as your album rotates.

Notifications

If you allow notifications on Android or iOS, your device registers an anonymous push token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android or Apple Push Notification service on iOS) linked only to your hashed room topic so Snug can wake your phone even when the app is closed. Push alerts contain only a generic action cue (for example, "new note" or "hug") and never include your message text, names, or photos. You can disable notifications at any time in the More tab or in system settings.

Permissions we ask for

  • Camera (optional) so you can add a photo to your shared album.
  • Photos (optional) so you can pick an existing image from your gallery.
  • Notifications (optional) so you can be notified of your partner's messages, hugs, and game turns even when the app is closed.

You can decline any of these; the rest of Snug will still work.

Third parties

Snug uses Cloudflare Workers to host its encrypted sync relay and, when notifications are enabled on mobile, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) or Apple Push Notification service (iOS) strictly to deliver push wake-ups. You can point Snug at your own ntfy-compatible server at any time from the More tab.

Children

Snug is intended for people 13 and older who are in a relationship together. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Use "Reset this device" in the More tab to unregister your push token and erase everything from your device. Encrypted sync frames on the relay automatically expire after up to thirty days.

Security caveats to know

  • Your couple code is your entire security. Keep it as private as your house key.
  • Anyone with your couple code (and the same sync server) can join your home. Change your sync server in the More tab and reset both devices if you ever need to rotate.
  • End-to-end encryption protects your data in transit, but if either device is compromised, so is the local copy of your data on it.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, when we can, mention it in the app.
